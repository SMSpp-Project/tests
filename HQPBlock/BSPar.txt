#
# The BlockSolverConfig of the tester of HQPBlock: one HQPSolver for each
# inner Solver and setting, all solving the same hierarchy in the same run,
# so that the tester compares each of them with the reference and hence with
# the others. The inner Solvers are DAQP and qpOASES (dense, active set),
# MOSEK and Clarabel (sparse, interior point), each of them in three
# settings:
#
#   - the rows of the lower levels relaxed and their slacks fixed, the
#     center of the regularization at the solution of the previous level
#     (HQPCfg.txt);
#   - the rows of the lower levels in the model with free slacks
#     (HQPCfg-free.txt);
#   - the center of the regularization at 0 (HQPCfg-prox0.txt).
#
# Each ComputeConfig is one of these three with the inner Solver changed;
# an HQPSolver whose inner Solver is not in the build is left out. The last
# two are DAQP and qpOASES in the first setting with each solve from scratch
# (InnerBSCfg-daqp-cold.txt, InnerBSCfg-qpoases-cold.txt), rather than from
# the previous solve of the same level, against which the batteries compare
# the iterations of the first and of the fourth.
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
