# The BlockSolverConfig of the father AbstractBlock of the Frank-Wolfe test
# of the suite, the least-squares fit in balls (whose oracle is BallSolver,
# BallBSCfg.txt) or the Wolfe dual on a box (BoxSolver, BoxBSCfg.txt).
#
# It registers the monolithic reference and one FrankWolfeSolver per
# variant of the direction, the way the BSPar of the suite do with the
# Solver of the SVMBlock: a single run cross-checks the variants against
# one another and all of them against the reference, a run stopped by
# intMaxIter being held to its bounds only.
#
# Every variant is an override of FWCfg.txt, which holds what they have in
# common. An override block writes the extra-Configuration slot even when
# it changes nothing of it: one that leaves it out is read on to the end of
# the stream and swallows the ComputeConfig that follows it.
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
