# A txt description of a BlockSolverConfig for a SVMBlock whose abstract
# representation encodes the Wolfe dual of the training problem, to be solved
# by three different Solver: a :MILPSolver on the abstract representation and
# the two that read the physical one, i.e., the ad hoc SMOSolver and
# LIBLINEAR.
#
# LIBLINEAR only trains the problems it can express, i.e., the linear kernel
# with the bias regularised, and it refuses the others rather than
# approximating them: this is why it has a BlockSolverConfig, and a batch, of
# its own rather than being one more Solver in BSPar.txt. Which problems those
# are is the complement of what LIBSVM has, both losses being fine here and
# only the linear kernel being so.
#
#   Donato Meoli
