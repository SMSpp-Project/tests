# ad hoc SMOSolver, which rather ignores the abstract representation and
# solves the training problem out of the data, and a LagrangianDualSolver,
# which relaxes the consensus constraints and solves the Lagrangian dual with
# a BundleSolver, each subproblem being the SVM of one chunk
