# A txt description of a BlockSolverConfig for a SVMBlock split into chunks
# the *Benders* way, i.e., with the model and the regularisation term left in
# the master and each chunk holding only the slacks of its own samples, to be
# solved by three different Solver: a :MILPSolver on the abstract
# representation of the whole thing, the ad hoc SMOSolver on the physical one,
# which knows nothing of the split, and BendersDecompositionSolver, which is
# the Solver the structure is there for.
#
# The three address the same training problem, hence they have to agree on its
# optimal value: the split is a structure of one instance and not another
# problem, which is the whole point of the comparison. This is the same
# cross-check BSPar-LD.txt does on the consensus structure, i.e., the other
# way of splitting the very same sum.
#
# The three can be attached to one Block because the Benders Solver is asked
# to give it back as it was at the end of each compute() [int_BDSlv_Restore in
# BDSCfg.txt]: it does not read the Block, it restructures it, grafting the
# root into a master of its own and taking the complicating Variable out of
# the Constraint of the chunks, and while that lasts the problem the others
# would find is not the one they were attached to. What the restoring costs is
# the reformulation, paid at every compute(), and the pool of cuts, which is
# made of pieces of the master and goes with it, so that nothing is warm
# started here.
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
