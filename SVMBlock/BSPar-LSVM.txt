# A txt description of a BlockSolverConfig for a SVMBlock whose abstract
# representation encodes the Wolfe dual of the training problem, to be solved
# by three different Solver: a :MILPSolver on the abstract representation and
# the two that read the physical one, i.e., the ad hoc SMOSolver and LIBSVM.
#
# LIBSVM only trains the problems it can express, i.e., the linear loss with
# the bias not regularised, the weight of the regularisation term 1 and any
# kernel but the Laplacian one, and it refuses the others rather than
# approximating them: this is why it has a BlockSolverConfig, and a batch, of
# its own rather than being one more Solver in BSPar.txt.
#
#   Donato Meoli
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
