# A BlockSolverConfig for the father AbstractBlock of the Frank-Wolfe test,
# whose leaves are the knapsacks of this suite, each of them solved by a
# dynamic programme as the Linear Minimization Oracle.
#
# It registers the monolithic reference and one FrankWolfeSolver per variant
# of the decomposition, the way the BSPar of the suite do with the Solver of
# its Block: the variants are variants of the same problem, so a single run
# cross-checks them against one another and all of them against the
# reference.
#
# Every variant is an override of FWCfg.txt, which holds what they have in
# common. An override block writes the extra-Configuration slot even when it
# changes nothing of it: one that leaves it out is read on to the end of the
# stream and swallows the ComputeConfig that follows it.
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
