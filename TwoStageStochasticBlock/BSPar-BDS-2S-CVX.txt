# A txt description of a BlockSolverConfig registering two Solver on the
# Benders form of a two-stage stochastic instance, i.e., on the Block the
# tester assembles around the instance the file gives: a :MILPSolver, which
# reads the whole tree and is therefore solving the extensive form, and
# BendersDecompositionSolver in its convex regime, where the value functions
# enter the Objective of the master as C05Function and a bundle drives the
# stabilized cutting-plane loop. This is the regime for a first stage that
# is continuous and decides something: BSPar-BDS-2S.txt, which uses the MILP
# one, takes from two to six times longer on the same instances.
#
# The two describe one problem, hence they have to agree on its optimal
# value, which is what the cross-check checks. They can stand on the same
# Block because the Benders Solver is asked to give it back as it was at the
# end of each compute() [int_BDSlv_Restore]: it does not read the Block, it
# restructures it, and while that lasts the problem the other Solver would
# find is not the one it was attached to.
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
