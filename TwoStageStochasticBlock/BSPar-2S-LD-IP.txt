# A txt description of a BlockSolverConfig for a TwoStageStochasticBlock,
# whose scenarios may be unit commitments, to be solved by four different
# Solver: a :MILPSolver on the deterministic equivalent as an integer
# problem, which gives the optimum, the recursive LagrangianDualSolver
# (LDrecCfg.txt), which relaxes the scenarios and the units within them at
# once and gives the bound of the dual, below the optimum by the duality gap,
# and the PrimalProximalHeur (PPHCfg.txt), which gives that bound and a
# feasible solution above the optimum, and the
# LagrangianDualRelaxationSolver with the same ComputeConfig, whose upper
# bound is the other side of its relaxation. The batch declares the dual and
# the latter relaxations and no tolerance for them and for the heuristic
# (-R ,,r,r -E ,inf,,inf), so that each is only required to bound the
# optimum on its side.
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

BlockSolverConfig     # exact type of the Configuration object

1  # the BlockSolverConfig is a "differential" one

4 # number of (the names of) Solver in this BlockSolverConfig

# now all the names of the Solver - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
GRBMILPSolver         # name of 1st Solver
PrimalProximalHeur    # name of 2nd Solver
LagrangianDualSolver  # name of 3rd Solver
LagrangianDualRelaxationSolver  # name of 4th Solver

4 # number of ComputeConfig in this BlockSolverConfig

# now all the ComputeConfig
# 1st ComputeConfig - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# ComputeConfig of the :MILPSolver, on the integer problem

*MILPCfg-IP.txt

# 2nd ComputeConfig - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# ComputeConfig of the PrimalProximalHeur, which gives a feasible solution

*PPHCfg.txt

# 3rd ComputeConfig - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# ComputeConfig of the recursive LagrangianDualSolver, which relaxes the
# scenarios and the units within them at once

*LDrecCfg.txt
*PPHCfg.txt

# end of BlockSolverConfig- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
