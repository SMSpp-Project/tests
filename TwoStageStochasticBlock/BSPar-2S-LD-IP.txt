# A txt description of a BlockSolverConfig for a TwoStageStochasticBlock
# whose scenarios are unit commitments, to be solved by four different
# Solver: a :MILPSolver on the deterministic equivalent as an integer
# problem, which gives the optimum, and three LagrangianDualSolver, which
# give the bound of the dual, below the optimum by the duality gap: the dual
# of the scenarios, each a MILP (LDIPCfg.txt), the nested one (LDLDCfg.txt)
# and the recursive one (LDrecCfg.txt). The batch declares the three duals as
# relaxations (-R ,r,r,r), so that each is only required to stay below the
# optimum.
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

BlockSolverConfig     # exact type of the Configuration object

1  # the BlockSolverConfig is a "differential" one

4 # number of (the names of) Solver in this BlockSolverConfig

# now all the names of the Solver - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
GRBMILPSolver         # name of 1st Solver
LagrangianDualSolver  # name of 2nd Solver
LagrangianDualSolver  # name of 3rd Solver
LagrangianDualSolver  # name of 4th Solver

4 # number of ComputeConfig in this BlockSolverConfig

# now all the ComputeConfig
# 1st ComputeConfig - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# ComputeConfig of the :MILPSolver, on the integer problem

*MILPCfg-IP.txt

# 2nd ComputeConfig - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# ComputeConfig of the LagrangianDualSolver of the scenarios, each a MILP

*LDIPCfg.txt

# 3rd ComputeConfig - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# ComputeConfig of the nested LagrangianDualSolver

*LDLDCfg.txt

# 4th ComputeConfig - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

*LDrecCfg.txt
