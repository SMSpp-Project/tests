# The ComputeConfig of a BundleSolver that is the cutting-plane method of
# Kelley: no stabilization (intMPStbl 3), whose master is the primal one in

# regime of the BendersDecompositionSolver it is compared with [see BDSMCfg-

ComputeConfig # exact type of the ComputeConfig object

intMPStbl         3 # no stabilization: the cutting-plane master of Kelley
intMPPrimal       1 # the primal master, the only one intMPStbl 3 has
intMPV2Form       1 # raw form: the Variable of the master are x itself
intMaxIter   100000 # max number of iterations for each call
intLogVerb        0 # log verbosity of the bundle
intBPar1    1000000 # no item is ever discarded, as no cut leaves the
                    # master of the MILP regime
intBPar2     100000 # max bundle size per component
intBPar3          1 # max n. of items to fetch from Fi() at each iteration
intBPar4          1 # min n. of items to fetch from Fi() at each iteration
intIntVars        1 # the integer Variable are kept integer

1  # number of double parameters

dblRelAcc      1e-8 # relative accuracy required to solution, i.e., the
                    # gap between the centre and the master
