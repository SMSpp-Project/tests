# The formulations that batch-mcf compares: the natural formulation, whose
# continuous relaxation is solved by a :MILPSolver, against its flow
# relaxation, an MCFBlock solved by every :MCFSolver, the LEMON ones first.
#
# The first formulation is the original Block, a pair of its BlockConfig and
# its BlockSolverConfig; each of the others is an R3Block of it, a vector
# of the Configuration that makes it [see get_R3_Block()], its BlockConfig
# and its BlockSolverConfig, which an UpdateSolver keeps up to date with the
# changes of the original Block; each R3Block is compared with the original
# Block [see -F of CFL_test].
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

2  # number of formulations: the Block, then its R3Block

# the original Block
SimpleConfiguration<std::pair<Configuration*,Configuration*>>
*CFLBCfg-NF.txt         # its BlockConfig
*CFLBSCfg-mcf.txt       # its BlockSolverConfig

# the 1st R3Block
SimpleConfiguration<std::vector<Configuration*>>
3  # the Configuration that makes it, its BlockConfig, its BlockSolverConfig
*R3BCfg-MCF.txt         # how it is made
*MCFBCfg.txt            # its BlockConfig
*MCFBSCfg-mcf.txt       # its BlockSolverConfig
