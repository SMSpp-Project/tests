# The variants that batch-cuts compares: the natural formulation with the
# strong forcing constraints, whose continuous relaxation is solved by a
# :MILPSolver, against a copy in the natural formulation whose relaxation
# separates them.
#
# The first variant is the original Block, a pair of its BlockConfig and
# its BlockSolverConfig; each of the others is an R3Block of it, a vector
# of the Configuration that makes it [see get_R3_Block()], its BlockConfig
# and its BlockSolverConfig, which an UpdateSolver keeps up to date with the
# changes of the original Block; each R3Block is compared with the original
# Block [see -F of CFL_test].
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

# the original Block
SimpleConfiguration<std::pair<Configuration*,Configuration*>>
*CFLBCfg-NF-SFsep.txt   # its BlockConfig
*CFLBSCfg-cuts.txt      # its BlockSolverConfig

# the 1st R3Block
SimpleConfiguration<std::vector<Configuration*>>
3  # the Configuration that makes it, its BlockConfig, its BlockSolverConfig
*R3BCfg-copy.txt        # how it is made
*CFLBCfg-NF-noSF.txt    # its BlockConfig
*CFLBSCfg-cuts-R3.txt   # its BlockSolverConfig
