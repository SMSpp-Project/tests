# Cross-check BlockSolverConfig for a SATBlock with a structure [see
# BPar-R.txt and BPar-D.txt]: the Solver of BSPar.txt, which see the whole
# instance whatever the structure, and the LagrangianDualSolver, which
# relaxes the rows of the father and solves the sub-SATBlock by OLL:
#
#   - CaDiCaLSATSolver      OLL on the clauses, with CaDiCaL
#   - MiniSATSolver         OLL on the clauses, with MiniSat, the SAT
#                           solver made anew as OLL grows
#   - :MILPSolver           the MILP formulation, sub-Block included
#   - BranchAndXSolver      an enumeration on the SATSolver with CaDiCaL
#   - LagrangianDualSolver  the Lagrangian dual (LDCfg.txt), a lower bound,
#                           which the batteries declare with -R
#
# It is BSPar.txt with the LagrangianDualSolver on top, which needs a
# structure: a SATBlock without one has no sub-Block to relax. The
# IntegralityBarrierSolver of BSPar.txt is not here, since the r of the
# clauses added to a sub-Block after the LagrangianDualSolver has been
# registered lose their weight in its Objective when the LagBFunction
# brings back the original costs, which the abstract copy that heuristic
# works on would read.
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
