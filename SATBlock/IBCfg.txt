# A ComputeConfig for the IntegralityBarrierSolver, a heuristic looking for
# a feasible point of the MILP formulation of a SATBlock by Frank-Wolfe on
# an integrality penalty divided by a barrier of the slacks: its runs of
# Frank-Wolfe are those of FWIBCfg.txt, and its oracle is a :MILPSolver on
# the linear relaxation (IBLPBSCfg.txt). Its upper bound is the value of the
# point it finds, +INF if it finds none, and its lower bound -INF, so the
# cross-check only holds it to the point being feasible and no better than
# the optimum.
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
