# A "meta"-BlockConfig: a map from a Block classname() to the BlockConfig to
# apply to every Block of that class. The tester dispatches it to the
# InvestmentBlock and over the whole inner Block of its InvestmentFunction,
# so it configures both regardless of where they sit:
#
#   InvestmentBlock         -> its bounds and the Solver of the inner Block,
#                              here the recursive Lagrangian dual
#                              (IBOCfg-LD.txt)
#   AbstractBlock           -> the same, for each component of an
#                              InvestmentBlock that has split its inner
#                              Block, which holds an InvestmentFunction of
#                              its own (IBOCfg-LD.txt)
#   ThermalUnitBlock        -> its formulation / P-C parameters (TUBCfg.txt)
#   DCNetworkBlock          -> its formulation selector (DCNBCfg.txt)
#   ACNetworkBlock          -> its scaling factors (ACBCfg.txt)
#   PolyhedralFunctionBlock -> its "linearized" representation (PFBCfg.txt)
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

# a "meta"-BlockConfig , i.e., a map classname --> its BlockConfig
SimpleConfiguration<std::map<std::string,Configuration*>>

6 # the number of elements in the map
InvestmentBlock         *IBOCfg-LD.txt
AbstractBlock           *IBOCfg-LD.txt

ThermalUnitBlock        *TUBCfg.txt
DCNetworkBlock          *DCNBCfg.txt
ACNetworkBlock          *ACBCfg.txt
PolyhedralFunctionBlock *PFBCfg.txt
