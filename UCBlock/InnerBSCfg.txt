# A "meta"-BlockSolverConfig: a map from a Block classname() to the
# BlockSolverConfig that says how to solve each inner sub-Block of the
# Lagrangian Dual. It is the value of the LagrangianDualSolver str_LagBF_BSCfg
# parameter (set in LDCfg.txt), and LagrangianDualSolver::default_BSCfg_for()
# dispatches it by classname() over the inner Block of each LagBFunction:
#
#   ThermalUnitBlock  -> TUBSCfg-CLP.txt (continuous relaxation of the unit)
#   *  (catch-all)    -> LPBSCfg.txt     (every other "hard" inner Block)
#
# The "*" entry is the default applied to any inner Block whose classname() is
# not listed explicitly, so a single (meta) str_LagBF_BSCfg can say "this Solver
# for the ThermalUnitBlock, that Solver for all the others", and the same scheme
# nests across Block levels (one map per level). Solving the inner subproblems in
# their continuous relaxation makes the Lagrangian Dual bound coincide with the
# continuous relaxation of the :MILPSolver on the whole UCBlock.
#
#   Donato Meoli
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

# a "meta"-BlockConfig , i.e., a map classname --> its BlockConfig
SimpleConfiguration<std::map<std::string,Configuration*>>

2 # the number of elements in the map

ThermalUnitBlock        *TUBSCfg-CLP.txt
*                       *LPBSCfg.txt
