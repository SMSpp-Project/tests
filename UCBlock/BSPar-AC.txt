# A txt description of a BlockSolverConfig for a UCBlock with an ACNetworkBlock,
# cross-checked by a :MILPSolver and a LagrangianDualSolver (using a
# BundleSolver as the inner Solver).
#
# Unlike the other BSPar variants this one does NOT attach the
# PrimalProximalHeur: on the AC (QCP) instances its configuration path
# triggers the known LagBFunction<->ThermalUnitBlock objective bug (the solve
# dies at once with "ThermalUnitBlock::add_Modification: the coefficients of
# the schedule-deviation variables cannot change"), so it stays out until
# that is fixed. It is BSPar-EASY.txt without the heuristic and with the two
# differences the AC case needs, both expressed as overrides of the shared
# *Cfg.txt fragments:
#
#   - the LP-reproducibility recipe for the :MILPSolver (Method, Presolve,
#     NumericFocus), since the AC LP relaxations are more degenerate than the
#     DC ones (SOCP cone + McCormick envelopes) and the default settings can
#     pick a different optimal vertex than the LagrangianDualSolver, producing
#     a small gap between the two;
#   - InnerBSCfg-AC.txt as the BlockSolverConfig of the LagBFunctions (the AC
#     ThermalUnitBlock is a QCP, solved by SCIP).
#
#   Enrico Gorgone, Antonio Frangioni, Donato Meoli
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
