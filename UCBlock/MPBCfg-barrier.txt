# The BlockSolverConfig of the :MILPSolver of the MasterProblemBlock of the
# BundleSolver on the pHydro instances of plan4res [see BSPar-DPb.txt]: that
# of MPBCfg.txt, with the barrier instead of the dual simplex. On these
# masters, with a cascade of reservoirs, the dual simplex is slow and it
# fails numerically (GUROBI's "Numeric error" on pHydro_18), so that the
# Lagrangian Dual does not converge in the 300 seconds of the battery on
# four of the five; with the barrier it converges on all of them in less
# than half of that, the residual of its reconstruction is below 1e-11, and
# the heuristic closes its gap on three of them. Elsewhere MPBCfg.txt keeps
# the dual simplex [see the comment to Method in MILPCfg-MPB.txt].
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
