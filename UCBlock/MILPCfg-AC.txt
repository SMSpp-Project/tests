# ComputeConfig of the :MILPSolver of the AC instances [see BSPar-AC.txt and
# BSPar-AC-24h.txt]: that of MILPCfg.txt with the GUROBI settings the AC
# relaxations need. They are convex QCPs with second-order cones, more
# degenerate than the DC LPs, so that the default settings can pick a
# different optimal point than the LagrangianDualSolver: the barrier without
# presolve and with the most careful numerics. NonConvex 0 says that the
# model is convex (GUROBI refuses it otherwise), so that the optimal value is
# also the lower bound GRBMILPSolver reports; with the default -1 it reports
# OBJBOUND, which on the 24h instances is 1.7% below the optimum.
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
