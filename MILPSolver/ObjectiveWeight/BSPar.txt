# A txt description of a BlockSolverConfig for the trees of the tester of
# the weight of the Objective whose Objective are partly quadratic: the
# :MILPSolver with a handling of their own of the Modification of the
# Objective (GRBMILPSolver, CPXMILPSolver, SCIPMILPSolver, MOSEKMILPSolver)
# and DAQPMILPSolver, which reads the objective of MILPSolver, all with
# their default parameters but the tolerances of SCIP and of MOSEK on a
# quadratic problem (SCIPCfg-QP.txt, MOSEKCfg-QP.txt). HiGHSMILPSolver is
# in BSPar-LP.txt only, since the active set QP solver of HiGHS (up to
# 1.15.1 at least) calls unbounded some of these convex QPs, whose
# variables are all bounded. The tester
# checks each Solver against the twin of the Block, a Solver that this
# build has not being left out.
