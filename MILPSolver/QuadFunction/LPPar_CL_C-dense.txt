# A txt description of a BlockSolverConfig for the Block of a model of the

# :MILPSolver for convex QPs, DAQPMILPSolver, QPOASESMILPSolver,
# ProxQPMILPSolver with its dense backend, which has no time limit and stalls

# QPMADMILPSolver and QuadProgMILPSolver with the regularized Hessian of

# not scale to the largest models of the class:
# batchCL_C_dense gives it to the tester with -S on the smaller ones, which
# the tester checks against the known optimal value.
