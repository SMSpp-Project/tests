# A BlockSolverConfig for the :MILPSolver of the MasterProblemBlock out of
# which FrankWolfeSolver takes the direction it gives the oracle when
# intFWDirection is eDirBundleMP [see strFWMPBCfg].
#
# The master is a QUADRATIC problem, small and re-solved at every iteration,
# so the backend has to be one that solves QPs: with HiGHS the run reports an
# error at every iteration and does not converge at all, while with Gurobi it
# does, which is why the backend is named here and not left to whichever one
# the rest of the suite happens to use. It is asked for one thread, so that
# the runs are comparable, and for an accuracy tighter than the one the
# decomposition is held to, the direction resting on it.
#
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
# - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
